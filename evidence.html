Evidence so far

HumeBot has been developed through controlled studies, each designed before it was run and each changing the design. None of them is the classroom qualification.

  • 60newly authored questions in a held-out study, prepared under an isolated authoring process and screened before execution
  • 238 / 238of the constrained system's citations in that study were exact, valid and supported by the evidence it selected
  • 50 / 60held-out questions receiving a verified answer
  • 1,205automated tests, including a 53-test regression suite for the current system
  • Frozensystem configuration, with an automated check that detects any change
  • 200questions in a formal qualification study designed prospectively — not yet written or run

What the numbers mean

Provenance held on unseen questions

In the 60-question held-out study, every one of the constrained system's 238 citations was Hume's exact wording, at a location that exists, supported by the evidence the answer actually relied on.

Availability is a separate requirement

The system gave a verified answer on 50 of the 60 held-out questions. The remaining 10 ended in visible non-answer states rather than being silently converted into unsupported answers. They are not counted as successful delivery. Provenance and answer availability are separate requirements, and the qualification study measures both.

Better prompting was not enough

A strongly prompted model given the whole text stopped inventing citation locations once told the citation format, but its quotations stayed roughly 86–92% exact across five samples. That is why quotation and provenance were placed under software control rather than trusted to the model.

A faculty audit found what automated review missed

A blinded audit of nine preselected response pairs by the project's faculty lead found both answers acceptable in five of nine, and identified two important omissions by the constrained system that automated review had not flagged — specific omissions, not pervasive interpretive failure. This is why the planned qualification includes a dedicated review for important omissions, and a faculty audit.

Development evidence is not qualification evidence

After the known failure types were addressed, the system gave a verified answer in 48 of 48 runs on the project's 24-question development set, run twice. That is development evidence only, not a qualification result. No qualification pass is claimed.

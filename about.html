About

HumeBot is a faculty-led academic project.

Its purpose is to give students reading David Hume's Enquiry concerning Human Understanding a supplemental resource that brings them closer to the primary text: answers that show the passages of Hume they rest on, exactly as Hume wrote them, and a visible "no verified answer" when the evidence does not support one. It complements the professor and the text; it does not replace either.

HumeBot is an academic project. It is not an official University service, and nothing on this site should be read as University sponsorship or endorsement.

The text

HumeBot's corpus is transcribed from the public-domain 1777 edition of Hume's Essays and Treatises on Several Subjects, with page-level provenance recorded for its units; the choice of canonical scan source is still under review. The project gratefully acknowledges Hume Texts Online, edited by Amyas Merivale and Peter Millican, whose scholarship informed the project and whose paragraph numbering HumeBot's passage identifiers follow.
